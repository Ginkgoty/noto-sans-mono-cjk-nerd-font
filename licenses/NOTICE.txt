Original font outlines © 2014–2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/).
Noto is a trademark of Google Inc. The original copyright and trademark notices
are retained in the fonts. The modified fonts are distributed under OFL 1.1.

This release adds resized icon outlines generated with Nerd Fonts 3.5.1,
converts those outlines to CFF and prunes cross-language locl substitutions.
Icon authors, source URLs and versions are listed in Icon-sets.md. The Font
Logos set in the upstream inventory is not part of this release.

Western outlines derive from Noto Sans Mono, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project
Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic), via the matching
Nerd Fonts 3.5.1 Regular and Bold builds. This release centers regional forms
in a 600/1200 grid, enlarges Han outlines by 3%, and rebuilds outlines in CFF.
See Noto-Sans-Mono-OFL.txt.
